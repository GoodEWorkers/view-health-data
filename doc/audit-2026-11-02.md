## Findings

### P1

**P1-1. Secondary text at 3.0 to 3.2:1** (Accessibility, Theming). Location: `src/iso/iso.css:85,91` (`.iso-plate-corner`, `.iso-legend`, read-only, override from `HospitalFloor.astro`), `src/pages/index.astro:226,247` (`.full-name`, panel `h3` and `h4`), `src/components/network/TraceDock.astro:47` (`h4`). Impact: the plate title, the plate hint, the readout, the whole Réseau legend (4 zones, 4 protocol families), panel section headings and the full service name are 10.5 to 14.4 px and fail 4.5:1 (3.24:1 on paper, 2.99:1 on the page ground). WCAG 1.4.3. Fix: `--anatomy-text` (7:1), as `DESIGN.md` already prescribes. Command: `/impeccable colorize`.

**P1-2. The packet loop forces layout and animates the invisible** (Performance). Location: `src/figures/hospital-floor/packets.ts` (`place` reads `getPointAtLength` then writes `cx`, `cy`, `r`, per packet, per frame) and `live.ts` (`sendPackets` starts packets on every layer but Salles, while Logiciels and Données set `--cable-show: 0` and Réseau is the only layer that shows them). Impact: 10 600 layouts and 10 700 style recalculations in 6 s on Réseau, and the same on Logiciels and Données with nothing visible; at 4x CPU the main thread is busy 4.9 s of every 6 s (Salles alone, with its idle tour: 3.3 s). Fix: read all positions first, then write; run packets only where cables show. Command: `/impeccable optimize`.

**P1-3. The fullscreen views are stage-sized boxes on phones** (Responsive, Accessibility). Location: `inspector.css` (`.inspector{position:absolute;inset:0}`), `viewsLive.ts` and `screensLive.ts` (`measure`: stage box minus 16 px), `networkViews.css:147` (stage `min-height` hack). Impact: at 390x844 the glossary is a 358x512 card inside the scrolling page showing two of 99 entries, the rule table is cut at the right edge and shows two rows, the close button is at the top, and nothing keeps focus inside (`aria-modal="false"`, siblings not inert). Evidence: `shots/audit-before-glossary-390-view.png`, `audit-before-rules-390-view.png`. Fix: true full-viewport sheets with their own scroll, a thumb-reachable close, background inert. Command: `/impeccable adapt`.

**P1-4. Touch has no preview step and the result is out of view** (Responsive). Location: `live.ts` (`pointerover` then `click`: a tap highlights and selects in one go; the tooltip is hidden for touch and by CSS under 720 px), `traceLive.ts` (a tap on a cable opens its sheet at once), `index.astro` and `SelectionBar.astro` (panel below the figure). Impact: with synthesized touch at 390x844, one tap on a room selects it and the panel head ends at y = 793 of 844 px: the visitor sees nothing change but a ring. There is no way to learn what a room is before committing, and the trace on Réseau (hover only) is unreachable. Fix: first tap highlights and shows the room card, second tap selects and brings the panel head into view. Command: `/impeccable adapt`.

**P1-5. The figure has no phone treatment** (Responsive). Location: `HospitalFloor.astro:177` (`overflow: hidden` under 560 px), `floorLayers.css:157` (labels hidden under 620 px), `iso.css:105`. Impact: the drawing is 370 px wide: windows are texture, device labels are removed, there is no pan and no hint that the page can be zoomed (pinch is native but undiscoverable). Evidence: `shots/audit-before-*-390-page.png`. Fix: a 720 px minimum drawing width in a horizontally scrollable stage, `touch-action` allowing pan and pinch, a one-line hint that goes away at the first gesture. Command: `/impeccable adapt`.

**P1-6. Réseau cables are 2.7 px wide targets** (Responsive). Location: `networkLayer.css:42` (`.cable-hit`, 3 viewBox px). Impact: on a 390 px phone a cable is about 2.5 CSS px wide, far under the 24 px minimum (WCAG 2.5.8; the panel's flow buttons are the equivalent control, which is why this is not P0). Fix: a 9 px hit stroke on coarse pointers. Command: `/impeccable adapt`.

## Patterns and systemic issues

- **One token misused as text colour**: `--anatomy-muted` is a decoration token (3.2:1) and carries text in six places; the kit stylesheet applies it to plate corners and the legend by default, so every figure built on the kit inherits the failure.
- **Hover is the only first-class input**: highlight, label, trace and tooltip are hover states, and touch reuses them by accident (`pointerover` fires before `click`). Selection, labels and trace need a touch path of their own.
- **Overlays are laid on the stage, not on the screen**: five views share one frame sized from the stage box; any viewport smaller than the stage's natural size breaks them together.
- **Per-frame work without a visibility check**: the packet loop and the tour tick run whether or not their output shows.
- **Breakpoints in px for layout that depends on text size** (header).

## Positive findings

- Colour is functional and tokenised: eight service colours from one file, CVD-tested pairs, `--room-text` derived for 4.5:1.
- Reduced motion keeps state: windows, editor text, cables and zones show in their final state, the tour stops, selection and focus flow still work; there is no global `0.01ms` kill.
- Names and roles: every control named, live regions for the overview sentence, the selection and the glossary count, roving tabindex on the layer tabs, `aria-pressed` on keys, Escape and focus return on every view.
- No overflow from 320 to 1919 px; the header collapses cleanly to two rows and scrolls the layer control sideways.
- Small JS (60 KB for all scripts), one self-hosted variable font in two subsets, no images, static output.
- Pure, tested transitions for the interaction model and the sequence clock.

## Recommended actions

2. **[P1] `/impeccable adapt`**: scrollable 720 px stage with hint, full-viewport sheets with inert background and a bottom close, two-tap touch model with a room card, panel head sticky and brought into view, 44 px targets, wider cable hit area, touch copy.

5. **[P3] `/impeccable polish`**: focus ring on the stage, heading levels in mock windows, header leading, straggler.
